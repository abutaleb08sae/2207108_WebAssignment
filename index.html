About TRY

Official Revelation: 21st February 2014

Our Story & Vision

TRY is a volunteer organization made up of KUETians with an indomitable desire and mentality to stand beside people. What started with the KUETians and the people around the campus has now expanded into impactful work across the entire country.

The main purpose of TRY is simply to make people smile by standing beside them. Working for humanity and endangered populations, each member of TRY embraces this humanitarian motivation for a lifetime.

How We Are Funded

Our initiatives are powered completely by the spirit of KUET. The Hall Collection, generous grants from KUET Alumni, and a small portion of the Student's Hall Dues form the core of TRY's donations.

Permanent

Core Initiatives

  • Distribution of winter clothes
  • Cycling to deliver blankets to helpless people
  • Iftar programs during Ramadan
  • Medical funds for sick KUETians
Active Response

Temporary Projects

  • Scholarships for underprivileged but talented students
  • Emergency flood victim relief operations
  • Immediate support to families in crisis
